Simple by design

Three steps.
No learning curve.

01 · Source

Give it useful text.

Clear factual material produces better basic questions. Names, dates, numbers, places and distinctive terms give the generator stronger answer candidates.

02 · Generate

It turns facts into choices.

The page identifies suitable terms and sentences, hides one answer and builds alternative options from the same source material.

03 · Review

You remain the final judge.

Attempt the quiz, check the score, view the source sentence behind each answer, then edit or use the material as appropriate.
